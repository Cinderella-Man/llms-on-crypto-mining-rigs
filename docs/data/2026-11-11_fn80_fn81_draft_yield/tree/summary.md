## Trees for a sequential drafter, against the chain of as many verified tokens

A *siblings* tree is the walk cut to L tokens with w - 1 leaves beside each of its first m tokens: the drafter's next most probable candidates there, from the passes that drafted the walk, so it needs no drafting pass more than the walk's L. Its value is exact: the walk is verified as a chain is, and on its first rejection the target's token there is matched against that position's leaves (a leaf that matches is accepted, and the pass has already computed the token after it). A *full* tree verifies every path over the w most probable candidates at each of the first m positions and goes on along the walk below its own node; it is an estimate: below a candidate off the walk it takes the walk's candidates (factorized), where the drafter would have drafted from that candidate. *nodes* is the verified tokens beyond the anchor, the chain's length for the same verify width; *tau* is accepted tokens + 1 per verify step on the steps whose walk reaches max(L, nodes); *se* is the standard error of the paired difference; *best-first* is the factorized best-first tree of as many nodes (an estimate); S = tau / (1 + passes x 0.0507 + nodes x r) in one-token steps.
