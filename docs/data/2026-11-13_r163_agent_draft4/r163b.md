# R163b: run 2 of Flash-Next's 4-token drafts (`v4rd42`, rig 2)

Q1483, 2026-11-13. This is the second run of R163's command, on rig 2, at the same time as R163's run 1 (`v4rd4`) on
rig 1. It gives the 4-token drafts two runs, one per rig, as `v4r` had. The tables for both runs are in this folder's
[README](README.md); this page adds run 2's own checks, the report over both arms, and the class.

**Class: a loss. Drafts of 3 stay in the release settings.**

Each step does yield about half a token more, as FN80 estimated. But each step takes about 30 ms longer, more than the
extra tokens pay for, so generation is slower on both rigs.

## Class

Two runs against two, one per rig, each rig paired with itself. The numbers are from the [README](README.md) and
[steps.tsv](steps.tsv).

| | rig 2: `v4r` | rig 2: **`v4rd42`** | rig 1: `v4r2` | rig 1: `v4rd4` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| equal work, min a task | 2.43 | **2.47** | 2.37 | 2.48 |
| generation tok/s | 26.67 | **24.99** | 27.61 | 24.47 |
| tokens a step | 3.2461 | **3.7760** | 3.2786 | 3.7305 |
| est. ms a step | 121.71 | **151.11** | 118.74 | 152.44 |

- **Generation:**
  - Rig 2 is 6.30 % slower and rig 1 11.37 % slower.
  - The gaps, 1.68 and 3.14 tok/s, are beyond the spread of `v4r`'s two runs (0.94) and of the two 4-token runs
    (0.52).
- **Equal work:** both 4-token runs (2.47 and 2.48 min a task) are slower than both 3-token runs (2.37 and 2.43).
- **Why:**
  - On rig 2, 16.3 % more tokens a step can pay for at most est. 19.87 ms more a step, and the step costs est. +29.40.
  - On rig 1, the README's est. 16.37 ms against est. +33.70.
  - FN80 had put the break-even at est. 14.77 ms.
- **Prompt speed** is not affected: 146.5 and 144.1 tok/s, against 144.6 and 133.9.

**Decision:** `--spec-draft-n-max 3` stays in `release/flash-next-v4/settings.env`.

The extra drafting pass costs est. 3.645 ms, so most of the extra time is likely the verify pass at 5 tokens. Several
of the preset's kernels act only at 2–4 tokens: DMT, `Q5K_MT`, `Q6K_MT` and `KCOL_SKIP` (Q1390 is Q6_K's read-once
kernel at 5–8 tokens). Q1484 profiles the 5-token verify on a P106 to find where the time goes. Drafts of 4 are worth
trying again once the extra cost a step falls under what the extra tokens pay for.
